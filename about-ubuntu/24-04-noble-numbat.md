## Release Date
Ubuntu 24.04 [was released](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/NobleNumbat/ReleaseSchedule){:title="Ubuntu 24.04 Noble Numbat Release Schedule"} on Thursday the 25th of April 2024. Ubuntu 24.04 Noble Numbat provides millions of users all over the world with a free, stable, secure operating system.

## Long Term Support
Ubuntu 24.04 Noble Numbat is a LTS release, and will be supported for 5 Years until [April 2029](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/Releases). This makes it ideal for users and organizations seeking stability and long-term support.
