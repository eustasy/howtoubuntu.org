# Privacy Policy

This website (howtoubuntu.org) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information about your visit, including:
- Pages viewed and time spent on the site
- Browser type and device information
- Referring website
- IP address (anonymized where possible)


## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may collect data and set cookies:
### GitHub Pages
This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical and usage data as described in their [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

### Cloudflare
We use Cloudflare for DNS and security. Cloudflare may collect technical and usage data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used in aggregate to improve the website. [Learn more about how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Google Search
We embed Google Custom Search to provide search functionality. Google may collect data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### YouTube Embeds
Some pages include embedded YouTube videos. YouTube (a Google service) may collect usage data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Social Media Buttons
We provide buttons to follow us on YouTube, Twitter, and Facebook. These services may collect data if you interact with the buttons. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:
- [YouTube](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Twitter](https://twitter.com/en/privacy)
- [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/policy.php)

## Cookies
howtoubuntu.org does not set or use cookies directly. However, third-party services listed above (such as Google Analytics, Google Search, YouTube, social media platforms, GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare) may use cookies as part of their operations. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or as described above for third-party services.

## Your Rights
You may have rights under data protection laws, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. For requests, please contact us at [contact@howtoubuntu.org](mailto:contact@howtoubuntu.org).

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
